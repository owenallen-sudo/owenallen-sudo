# My ethics
- I refuse to make code that isn't open-source.
- I much prefer small, auditable and comprehensible codebases over "feature-rich" (bloated) alternatives.
- I have several times read over NASA's power of 10 but don't implement it due to impracticality.
- I like to avoid setjmp, longjmp, goto and recursion but will use them if necessary.
- I'll also soon start doing most my compiling c in -Wpedantic mode.
- I prefer to think of the unix philosophy 'do one thing and do it well' and 'everything is a file' as 'Make it as extensible and composable as possible, with a simple base all over as unified of a protocol as possible. So long as you don't introduce significant drawbacks, that is.', whether that protocol is namespaces or whatever you choose.
